<h2>About the Role</h2>
<p>Cloudflare’s Data Org builds the systems that ingest, process, store, and retrieve data generated across our global network. These systems power the logs, analytics, and alerts that give customers near real-time visibility into the security, reliability, and performance of their Internet properties.</p>
<p><strong>&nbsp;</strong></p>
<p>We are looking for systems-oriented backend engineers who enjoy solving distributed-systems problems at large scale. You will build primarily in Go, operate the systems you create, and work across the data lifecycle, from high-throughput ingestion and delivery to analytical storage and customer-facing retrieval.</p>
<p><strong>&nbsp;</strong></p>
<p>Engineers in this role typically develop depth in one of two areas:</p>
<ol>
	<li>Analytical database systems: Building and operating our ClickHouse-based analytical platform, improving database performance, reliability, and scalability.</li>
	<li>Data delivery and retrieval: Building streaming pipelines, public APIs, log delivery integrations, and alerting systems that make customer data accessible and actionable.</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>&nbsp;</strong></p>
<p>We are looking for strong backend and systems fundamentals, production operational experience, and demonstrated depth in at least one of these areas.</p>
<h2>What You’ll Do</h2>
<ul>
	<li>Design, build, and operate reliable distributed systems that process very large volumes of data.</li>
	<li>Develop and optimise backend services and data platform components, primarily in Go.</li>
	<li>Improve data integrity, delivery latency, query performance, availability, and operational efficiency.</li>
	<li>Build systems that handle failure safely through techniques such as backpressure, retries, idempotency, replication, and graceful degradation.</li>
	<li>Define service-level objectives, monitor production health, plan capacity, and respond to incidents.</li>
	<li>Diagnose performance bottlenecks across ingestion, processing, storage, and retrieval.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Depending on your area of focus:</strong></p>
<ul>
	<li>Build and operate large-scale ClickHouse clusters and contribute improvements to the ClickHouse ecosystem.</li>
	<li>Develop data delivery pipelines that route data from Cloudflare’s global network to customer destinations.</li>
	<li>Build and scale GraphQL APIs, log delivery integrations, and near real-time alerting products.</li>
	<li>Collaborate with product and engineering teams to understand their data needs and design pragmatic solutions.</li>
	<li>Evaluate technologies and contribute to the next generation of Cloudflare’s data platform.</li>
	<li>Participate in an on-call rotation for the production systems owned by your team.</li>
</ul>
<h2>What We’re Looking For</h2>
<ul>
	<li>At least three years of professional software engineering experience building backend, infrastructure, database, or distributed-data systems.</li>
	<li>Strong Go programming skills, or substantial systems programming experience (Rust, C++) with the ability to become productive in Go quickly.</li>
	<li>Experience building and operating production services, including deployment, monitoring, capacity planning, incident response, and debugging.</li>
	<li>A solid understanding of distributed-systems concepts, including concurrency, consistency, fault tolerance, partitioning, and data integrity.</li>
	<li>Experience with high-throughput or low-latency data processing.</li>
	<li>Hands-on experience with observability tools such as Prometheus and Grafana, including monitoring high-cardinality workloads.</li>
	<li>Strong analytical and troubleshooting skills, particularly for complex production problems.</li>
	<li>Clear communication and an ability to collaborate across engineering and product teams.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Demonstrated depth in at least one of the following areas:</strong></p>
<ul>
	<li>Database systems: SQL, analytical databases, database internals, query optimisation, performance tuning, or operating large database clusters.</li>
	<li>Data delivery and retrieval: Streaming systems, event-driven architectures, data pipelines, API development, or customer-facing data integrations.</li>
</ul>
<h2>Helpful Experience</h2>
