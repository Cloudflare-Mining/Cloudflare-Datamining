<h3><strong>About the Role</strong></h3>
<p>As the Senior Engineering Manager for Cloudflare Registry and Cloudflare Registrar, you will lead the core engineering team responsible for two foundational columns of Cloudflare’s ecosystem. Together, Cloudflare Registrar (servicing over 2 million customers with a current offering of over 430 domain extensions for purchase) and Cloudflare Registry (powering .gov hosting) enable seamless domain management and top-level domain hosting at massive scale across the global web.</p>
<p>Your mission will be to architect, scale, and innovate across the complete domain lifecycle—from purchasing across 430+ supported domain extensions and DNS integration via Registrar, to robust .gov hosting infrastructure, compliance, and registry operations via Registry.</p>
<p>You will manage a distributed, high-velocity team operating at the intersection of core Internet infrastructure, regulatory compliance, and massive system scale. In this high-visibility role, you will partner closely with Product Management, Security, and cross-functional platform teams to ensure our domain pipeline is rock-solid, highly available, and ready for the next generation of web infrastructure.</p>
