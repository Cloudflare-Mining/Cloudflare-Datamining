<h2>About the team</h2>
<p>Cloudflare is redefining the way networks are run at scale, and some of the largest networks in the world now run their traffic through us. Companies connect their data centers, offices, and clouds to Cloudflare, and our network carries, routes, and protects that traffic from hundreds of cities around the world.</p>
<p>Our teams build the systems that make those connections work: the APIs customers use to describe their networks, the control planes that turn that into configuration on every server we run, the health checks that move traffic away from failing paths, and the BGP sessions we run with customer networks (the systems behind products like Magic Transit and Cloudflare WAN). We are a team of engineers and product managers who obsess over quickly building products that solve real customer problems, using techniques that take advantage of Cloudflare's unique architecture and global network footprint.</p>
<h2>About the role</h2>
<p>We're looking for engineers who like hard distributed systems problems: keeping state consistent across hundreds of data centers, changing configuration safely while traffic is flowing, and deciding what to do when parts of the system fail. You don't need to be a networking expert; we'll help you learn the rest.</p>
<p>Some of the things you'd work on:</p>
<ul>
	<li>The APIs and control plane services that take a customer's network configuration (tunnels, routes, health checks) and push it to every server in our network, quickly and safely</li>
	<li>Tunnel health checks that run from every data center, and the logic that reroutes customer traffic when a path degrades</li>
	<li>An anycast BGP implementation where customer sessions survive being moved between servers and data centers, built partly on Cloudflare's own developer platform</li>
	<li>Experimental tooling that catch routing flaps and failures before customers do</li>
	<li>Getting logs and analytics back to customers so they can see what their networks are doing</li>
</ul>
<p>You'll build, deploy, and operate these systems on top of one of the largest edge networks in the world. You'll partner with some of the best engineers and product managers, on our teams and across Cloudflare, to solve big problems that have a meaningful impact in the lives of our customers and users.</p>
<p>We're looking for curious, empathetic engineers who are excited to join us in changing the way networking is done!</p>

<ul>
	<li>Experience designing, building, and operating distributed systems: consistency, replication, failure handling, and safe rollouts</li>
