<p><strong>About the team</strong></p>
<p><a href="https://www.cloudflare.com/zero-trust/products/browser-isolation/">Cloudflare Browser Isolation</a> runs web content in remote browsers, helping protect users from threats while preserving a responsive browsing experience. Our team builds the Chromium-based browsing engine, remote rendering and streaming technology, and distributed services that manage browser sessions across Cloudflare’s global network.</p>
<p>You will work at the intersection of systems programming, distributed systems, and security. Engineers contribute across the product, collaborating on browser changes, service orchestration, observability, and build infrastructure.</p>
<p><strong>Scope and growth</strong></p>
<p>At this level, you will be accountable for outcomes across a problem area that may span several components. You will establish technical direction, make uncertainty and trade-offs explicit, and help other engineers succeed while remaining hands-on. This is an individual-contributor role; people-management experience is not required.</p>
<p><strong>Technologies we use</strong></p>
<ul>
	<li>Browser engine: Chromium and C++.</li>
	<li>Systems programming: Rust.</li>
	<li>Distributed services and orchestration: Go, TypeScript, Cloudflare Workers, Nomad, and Consul.</li>
	<li>Remote rendering and streaming: WebRTC and Skia.</li>
	<li>Browser client: TypeScript, WebAssembly, and WebGL.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>What you will do</strong></p>
<ul>
	<li>Lead complex, multi-component initiatives from problem definition and design through implementation, staged rollout, and measurable production outcomes.</li>
	<li>Design and implement changes in our Chromium-based C++ browser and the distributed services and systems components supporting remote browsing, working across Go, Rust, and TypeScript.</li>
	<li>Make and explain architectural trade-offs across latency, availability, resource efficiency, security boundaries, and operational complexity.</li>
	<li>Investigate difficult cross-layer failures and performance bottlenecks, distinguishing browser, host, network, and control-plane causes.</li>
	<li>Improve resilience, capacity management, observability, and release safety; define success metrics and validate results.</li>
	<li>Coordinate work with partner teams, surface dependencies and risks early, and guide delivery through ambiguity.</li>
	<li>Mentor engineers and raise the quality of code reviews, design reviews, testing, documentation, and operational practices.</li>
	<li>Participate in the team’s production on-call rotation after onboarding, lead technical incident investigations as needed, and drive durable corrective actions.</li>
</ul>
<p>This role may require flexibility to be on-call outside of standard working hours to address technical issues as needed.</p>
<p><strong>Desirable Skills, Knowledge, and Experience</strong></p>
<ul>
	<li>Strong production C++ proficiency, including memory and resource ownership, concurrency, and debugging in substantial codebases.</li>
	<li>A track record of independently leading complex systems or distributed-service improvements across multiple components, from an unclear problem to a reliable production outcome.</li>
	<li>Strong Linux/UNIX and networking fundamentals, with experience diagnosing correctness, latency, and resource-consumption issues using profiling and operational evidence.</li>
	<li>Experience designing and operating distributed systems, with sound judgment about service boundaries, data partitioning, consistency, timeouts and retries, backpressure, capacity limits, and recovery from partial failures.</li>
	<li>Experience designing safe rollout and recovery strategies and maintaining production software over its lifecycle.</li>
	<li>Ability to communicate trade-offs clearly, align stakeholders, mentor teammates, and improve engineering practices through constructive feedback.</li>
	<li>A willingness to work across C++, Rust, Go, and TypeScript and learn unfamiliar parts of the stack.</li>
</ul>
