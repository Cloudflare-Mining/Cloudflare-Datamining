<p><strong>About the Role</strong></p>
<p>The Product Platform Tools team builds and operates Cloudflare's internal support and admin platform, which customer-facing and operational teams across the company use to do their jobs. It includes the API gateway and authorization layer that control access to those teams' internal applications.</p>
<p>We're hiring a hands-on backend engineer to build and evolve the services, APIs, and gateway for the platform. You'll set a high standard for how applications onboard onto the platform, taking an API-driven approach to contracts, access, and integration. You'll work with the engineering teams who onboard their apps and with the non-engineering teams who use the platform daily. You'll need curiosity about how Cloudflare works under the hood, a security-first mindset, and the communication skills to turn operational needs into reliable platform capabilities.</p>
<p><strong>Responsibilities</strong></p>
<ul>
	<li><strong>API and gateway development: </strong>design and build the backend services, APIs, and gateway capabilities (routing, authentication, request handling, upstream integration) at the core of the internal admin platform.</li>
	<li><strong>API-driven onboarding: </strong>set and raise the standard for onboarding applications onto the platform. Use clear API contracts, well-governed access, and a repeatable integration path so teams can onboard quickly and consistently.</li>
	<li><strong>Stakeholder partnership: </strong>work directly with Support, Legal, and Security to turn operational needs into maintainable platform features.</li>
	<li><strong>Security and authorization: </strong>own the platform's authorization model. Work with Security and Legal to implement and enforce access control, governance, and compliance requirements in code.</li>
	<li><strong>Reliability of what you build: </strong>build observability and resilience into your services, take part in on-call for them, respond to incidents, and lead post-incident reviews.</li>
	<li><strong>Engineering excellence: </strong>ramp quickly into unfamiliar and legacy codebases and make incremental improvements that reduce toil. Keep a high bar through code review and design docs.</li>
	<li><strong>AI-assisted engineering: </strong>use AI coding tools as a normal part of how you design, build, and debug. Review their output with the same rigor you'd apply to any other code.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Desirable Skills, Knowledge, and Experience</strong></p>
<p><em>These are examples of what a strong candidate brings:</em></p>
<p><strong>Must-Have Skills</strong></p>
<ul>
	<li><strong>Professional experience: </strong>5+ years building and operating production backend services at scale.</li>
	<li><strong>Languages: </strong>strong proficiency in Go and/or TypeScript.</li>
	<li><strong>API gateway and API design: </strong>hands-on experience building or operating an API gateway or similar edge/proxy layer, and designing REST APIs. This covers routing, request lifecycle, authentication, and versioning.</li>
	<li><strong>Authentication and authorization: </strong>strong understanding of OAuth 2.0, OIDC, JWT, RBAC, and policy-based access (such as OPA/Rego), with a security-first approach to design.</li>
	<li><strong>Data: </strong>hands-on experience with PostgreSQL and Redis, including schema design, query optimization, and caching strategies.</li>
	<li><strong>Cross-team collaboration: </strong>comfortable working with engineering, Security, Legal, and non-technical stakeholders, and explaining technical detail to each.</li>
	<li><strong>Ownership and autonomy: </strong>confident in legacy code, self-directed, and able to drive ambiguous problems to resolution across team boundaries.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Nice-to-Have Skills</strong></p>
