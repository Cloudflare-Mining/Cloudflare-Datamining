<p><strong>About the role</strong></p>
<p>The web is undergoing a structural transition from a search-first economy to an AI-mediated, agentic ecosystem. Cloudflare sits in front of more than 20% of the world's web traffic, providing an unmatched, front-row view of how Large Language Models (LLMs), AI bots, and autonomous agents interact with digital content.</p>
<p>We are seeking a <strong>Strategic Partnerships Lead / Director</strong> to join Cloudflare’s Strategic Partnerships team. In this high-impact, zero-to-one role, you will define and execute our strategy to transform Cloudflare’s rich network and CDN data into actionable business, marketing, and Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) intelligence.</p>
<h4><strong>What you'll do</strong></h4>
<p>You will act as the crucial bridge between enterprise customers, Product &amp; Engineering, and Go-To-Market (GTM) teams. Your mission is to build and commercialize the "Analytics of the Agentic Era"—helping non-technical business leaders, CMOs, and digital publishers measure content value, track agentic traffic, and optimize brand visibility across AI answer engines.</p>
<p><strong>Responsibilities</strong></p>
<p><strong>1. Product Influence &amp; Analytics Suite Definition</strong></p>
<ul>
	<li>Partner closely with Product Management and Engineering to translate raw CDN, bot, and network traffic signals into executive-ready dashboards and commercial analytics products.</li>
	<li>Help design first-party analytics tools tailored for business personas—focusing on metrics like scraping-to-referral ratios, prompt library integrations, LLM brand presence, and content gap analysis.</li>
	<li>Run design partner programs with top enterprise customers and media brands to gather feedback, validate feature roadmaps, and co-develop analytics prototypes.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>2. GTM Strategy &amp; Sales Enablement</strong></p>
<ul>
	<li>Collaborate with Cloudflare’s Enterprise Sales and GTM teams to package, price, and position the Advanced Analytics Suite for commercial buyers.</li>
	<li>Create sales playbooks, value frameworks, and pitch materials that translate complex technical infrastructure data into compelling business stories for non-technical buyers.</li>
	<li>Serve as a domain expert in high-stakes customer meetings, executive briefing centers (EBCs), and pilot programs.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>3. Expanding the Buyer Horizon Beyond CISOs</strong></p>
<ul>
	<li>Champion Cloudflare’s strategic positioning beyond security (CISOs) and infrastructure (CTOs), actively expanding our mindshare among CMOs, digital strategists, and commercial leaders.</li>
	<li>Educate external stakeholders on critical concepts in the agentic web, such as Answer Engine Optimization (AEO), Generative Engine Optimization (GEO), and synthetic web traffic attribution.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>4. Ecosystem &amp; Strategic Alliances</strong></p>
<ul>
	<li>Identify, evaluate, and structure strategic partnerships with adjacent players in the marketing tech and AI ecosystem (e.g., AEO measurement platforms, attribution providers, AI ad networks, and commercial data platforms).</li>
	<li>Leverage ecosystem relationships to enrich Cloudflare’s analytics capabilities and build joint go-to-market motions.</li>
</ul>
<h4><strong>Desirable Skills, Knowledge, and Experience:</strong></h4>
<ul>
	<li><strong>10+ Years of Experience:</strong> Demonstrated track record in strategic partnerships, business development, product marketing, or strategy within digital media, martech, web analytics, or data platform ecosystems.</li>
	<li><strong>Technical + Commercial Fluency:</strong> Exceptional ability to understand complex technical infrastructure (CDNs, bot management, traffic logs, APIs) while possessing a keen empathy for marketing and business mechanics (KPIs, CAC/LTV, attribution, content monetization).</li>
	<li><strong>0-to-1 Builder Mindset:</strong> Proven experience taking early-stage, ambiguous product concepts from ideation to scaled commercial distribution.</li>
	<li><strong>C-Suite Storytelling &amp; Communication:</strong> Superior written and verbal communication skills, with a track record of presenting to C-level executives (CMOs, CEOs) and simplifying complex developer concepts into strategic business imperatives.</li>
	<li><strong>Cross-Functional Leadership:</strong> Strong experience orchestrating alignment across engineering, product management, sales, legal, and executive leadership teams.</li>
	<li><strong>Location:</strong> Based in or willing to work hybrid from our <strong>San Francisco, CA</strong> or <strong>New York, NY</strong> or <strong>Austin, TX </strong>offices.</li>
