<h2>Role Summary</h2>
<p>As a Senior Strategic Partner Manager, you will work directly with Product, Engineering, and executive leadership to identify, build, and launch key partnerships that accelerate our product roadmap and drive platform adoption.</p>
<p>The initiatives you own will span strategic co-build integrations, OEM relationships, and complex GTM alliance build-outs. This is a hands-on execution role for a versatile problem-solver—you will own partnerships end-to-end, moving from initial strategic alignment through technical scoping, contract negotiation, integration, and go-to-market launch.</p>
<p>You will flex across different technology domains as company priorities shift, diving into technical details, validating integration use cases, and bringing cross-functional teams (Legal, Security, GTM, Finance) together to deliver real outcomes.</p>
<h2>Role Responsibilities</h2>
<ul>
	<li>Identify, evaluate, and structure strategic partnerships (technology integrations, ecosystem plays, and go-to-market alliances) across Cloudflare's product areas, prioritizing based on where the business needs it most.</li>
	<li>Partner directly with Product and Engineering leaders to assess technical fit, integration approach, and roadmap alignment for prospective partnerships.</li>
	<li>Build the business case for each partnership: sizing the opportunity, validating the underlying use case, and making a clear, evidence-based recommendation on whether to pursue it.</li>
	<li>Lead negotiation and execution end-to-end, from initial scoping through signed agreement and launch.</li>
	<li>Act as the connective tissue between partner organizations and internal stakeholders (Product, Engineering, Legal, Marketing, Sales) to ensure both organizations deliver outcomes.&nbsp;</li>
	<li>Move fluidly across product areas and partner types as priorities shift, ramping up on new technical domains quickly and independently.</li>
	<li>Demonstrate deep technical fluency. You must be able to independently evaluate technical documentation and engage credibly with engineers, rather than relying on others to translate how a partner’s technology works.&nbsp;</li>
</ul>

	<li>Technical fluency: able to understand product architecture and engineering trade-offs well enough to independently evaluate partnership and integration opportunities, not just relay them.</li>
	<li>Proven ability to navigate unstructured, open-ended problems with an attention to detail to close and launch complex deals.&nbsp;</li>
	<li>A track record of aligning senior stakeholders across product, engineering, legal, GTM and partner organizations around competing priorities, keeping teams accountable, and pushing projects across the finish line.&nbsp;</li>

	<li>Ability to pick up new technical domains quickly and shift focus as business priorities evolve.&nbsp;</li>
