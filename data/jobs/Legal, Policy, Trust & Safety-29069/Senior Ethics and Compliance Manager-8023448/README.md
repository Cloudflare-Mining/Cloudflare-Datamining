<p><strong>What You’ll Do</strong></p>
<p>As the world’s first connectivity cloud, Cloudflare helps connect and protect millions of customers globally. Everyone from individuals to the world’s largest enterprises use our unified platform of networking, security, and developer services to succeed everywhere in the world. Supporting a global platform of this scale requires a legal team that moves fast. That’s why at Cloudflare, we don’t just manage risk—we clear the road for global innovation. We are building a next generation compliance program that leverages AI technology to scale our routine tasks, allowing our team members to focus on the proactive work of fostering a culture of compliance.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p>We are looking for a talented, experienced, and energetic compliance professional&nbsp; to join our legal team as a Senior Ethics and Compliance Manager (EMEA) based in London. You will play a key role executing the day-to-day nuts and bolts of our legal compliance within the EMEA region, including internal investigations, managing conflicts of interest disclosures, gifts and entertainment reviews, legal and compliance training, and project management of cross-functional initiates. Just as importantly, you will be responsible for helping build our systems and processes in a way that leverages AI to allow us to do our best work. The ideal candidate is passionate about compliance and can channel that passion into solving complex problems and novel legal issues in a fast-paced environment. As part of the job, you will:</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p><strong>Responsibilities:</strong></p>
<ul>
	<li>Support Cloudflare’s <strong>internal investigations program</strong>, including management of investigation case files, conducting witness interviews, collecting supporting documentation, and drafting final reports;</li>
	<li>Help design and implement <strong>policies and controls</strong> to ensure that Cloudflare is protecting its customers and employees, while satisfying our legal obligations;</li>
	<li>Assist with Cloudflare’s <strong>conflict of interest disclosure process</strong>, including review and approval of potential employee conflicts and gift and entertainment requests<strong>;</strong></li>
	<li>Assist with the administration of Cloudflare’s <strong>compliance training and education program</strong>, including the design of regional compliance training content, target audience identification and effectiveness measurement;</li>
	<li>Assist with execution of a regular integrity and <strong>compliance communications strategy within the region</strong>; and</li>
	<li>Project manage a variety of other <strong>cross-functional compliance initiatives</strong>.</li>
</ul>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p>The above list is representative and additional responsibilities may be assigned, as required by the need for the compliance program to support Cloudflare’s Legal team with agility in a dynamic environment.</p>
