<h3><strong>Available Location</strong>: Austin, TX&nbsp;</h3>
<h3><strong>Job Title</strong></h3>
<p>Senior Oracle Supply Chain Specialist</p>
<h3><strong>Role Summary</strong></h3>
<p>The Senior Oracle Supply Chain Specialist provides leadership and oversight for the implementation, integration, and performance of the Oracle Fusion Cloud ERP platform, with a primary focus on Supply Chain Management functional areas and the underlying integration layer. Reporting directly to the Oracle CoE Leader, this role acts as the primary functional escalation point for critical system stability and architectural design decisions. Additionally, you will collaborate with senior leaders across Operations, Infrastructure, and Finance to align the Oracle technology roadmap with overarching organizational growth goals.</p>
<h3><strong>Role Responsibilities</strong></h3>
<ul>
	<li>Serve as the ultimate functional authority for all post-implementation Oracle Fusion Product Management and Supply Chain Management modules, including Product Development, Product Information Management, Procurement, Inventory Management, Cost Management, Logistics, and Contract Manufacturing/Planning integrations.</li>
	<li>Own the design and continuous evolution of complex supply chain processes—such as global source-to-pay, inventory optimization, and manufacturing execution interfacing—to ensure solutions remain scalable, compliant, and focused on leveraging out-of-the-box Fusion capabilities.</li>
	<li>Oversee post-go-live performance tuning and optimization initiatives across all core supply chain management business processes.</li>
	<li>Define and enforce technical standards for cross-application integration using Oracle Integration Cloud (OIC) to ensure reliable, secure, and high-volume data exchange between Oracle Fusion, legacy NetSuite systems, WMS, and other critical operational platforms.</li>
	<li>Manage non-functional requirements within the Oracle Cloud environment, encompassing security architecture, role design governance, performance benchmarking, monitoring strategies, and the execution of quarterly updates.</li>
	<li>Direct the overarching strategy for data quality, data conversion, and ongoing master data governance across all core supply chain entities including Items, Suppliers, and Locations.</li>
</ul>
<h3><strong>Role Requirements (Must-Have Skills)</strong></h3>
<ul>
	<li>8+ years of professional experience managing and supporting supply chain management systems.</li>
	<li>5+ years of hands-on experience implementing or managing Oracle Fusion Cloud ERP, with verifiable success leading the Supply Chain Management track through a full lifecycle implementation.</li>
	<li>Demonstrated success in establishing IT governance, managing operational risks, and ensuring technical compliance (such as SOX controls) within a cloud ecosystem.</li>
	<li>Exceptional executive communication, stakeholder management, and cross-functional team development abilities.</li>
	<li>Active certifications in relevant Oracle Fusion Cloud Application modules.</li>
</ul>
<h3><strong>Nice-to-Have Skills</strong></h3>
<ul>
	<li>Experience participating in major ERP migration programs, particularly migrating from legacy ERP environments.</li>
	<li>Familiarity with electronic manufacturing/assembly environments and direct experience integrating shop-floor systems with Oracle Fusion Manufacturing/Inventory.</li>
	<li>Working knowledge of AI Agent Studio for Oracle Fusion Cloud Applications.</li>
	<li>Hands-on experience with Oracle Fusion Global Trade Management systems.</li>
</ul>
