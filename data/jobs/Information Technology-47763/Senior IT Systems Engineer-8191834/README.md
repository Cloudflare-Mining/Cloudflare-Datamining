<h3 id="about-the-role">About the Role</h3>
<p>Cloudflare's IT Asset Management (ITAM) team is building the future of how Cloudflare runs itself — and we're doing it on Cloudflare. We are rearchitecting our internal IT operations off third-party tools and onto Cloudflare's own platform: Workers, Durable Objects, D1, AI Gateway, and the broader stack. The goal is to replace what we buy with what we build, prove that Cloudflare is its own best customer, and create a model for how internal IT platforms should work.</p>
<p>As the Senior IT Systems Engineer on the IT Platform &amp; Automation pillar, you are the architect of that future. You will own the design and build of Cloudflare-native systems that replace today's third-party tooling — starting with MCP servers that connect our IT workflows to Cloudflare OS, and expanding to full application refactors that bring asset management, software inventory, and operational automation onto the platform natively.</p>
<p>You should understand how today's systems work — not because maintaining them is the job, but because you need to know what you're replacing and why. The job is to build what comes next.</p>
<p>This is a high-ownership, high-autonomy IC role. You will operate with significant independence, define your own technical approach, and serve as the engineering bar-setter for the pillar.</p>
<hr>
<h3 id="what-youll-do">What You'll Do</h3>
<ul>
	<li>Architect and build Cloudflare-native replacements for ITAM's current third-party tooling — own the full technical design from first principles.</li>
	<li>Build and own ITAM's internal MCP servers: the integration layer that connects IT systems, data sources, and workflows to Cloudflare OS and AI-powered tooling.</li>
	<li>Refactor existing IT operational workflows onto Cloudflare's platform — Workers, Durable Objects, D1, AI Gateway, Queues, and related primitives.</li>
	<li>Build AI agents and AI-powered automation that make IT operations faster, more accurate, and less manual — wired natively into the platform you're building.</li>
	<li>Define the data architecture for ITAM's future platform: clean ownership models, reliable source-of-truth design, and integrations that don't require third-party middleware.</li>
	<li>Lead build-vs-buy decisions with a clear bias toward building on Cloudflare where it makes sense — and retiring third-party tools when the Cloudflare-native version is ready.</li>
	<li>Partner with the Software Asset Management (SAM) pillar to understand their operational requirements and build the platform they'll run on.</li>
	<li>Set engineering standards for the pillar: architecture, code quality, documentation, and how decisions get made and recorded.</li>
	<li>Mentor junior and mid-level engineers on the pillar; raise the bar through design reviews and direct technical coaching.</li>
	<li>Own the security, compliance, and data governance posture of the systems you build.</li>
</ul>
