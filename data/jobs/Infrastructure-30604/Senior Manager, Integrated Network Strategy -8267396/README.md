<h3><strong>Role Summary</strong></h3>
<p>Cloudflare is seeking an experienced, action-oriented leader to serve as the primary strategic network lead for our Latin America (LATAM) infrastructure footprint.</p>
<p>This role acts as a critical liaison between Cloudflare and LATAM’s major Internet Service Providers (ISPs), telecom operators, commercial datacenter providers, and transport carriers.</p>
<p>You will provide strategic leadership and regional expertise to align network growth, scale infrastructure capacity, and support high-performing digital services across the region.</p>
<h3><strong>What You’ll Do</strong></h3>
<ul>
	<li><strong>Executive Partnership Alignment:</strong> Build and maintain strategic relationships with executive stakeholders across top regional ISPs, telecommunications operators, and infrastructure partners to drive scalable network alignment and long-term stability.</li>
	<li><strong>Capacity Scaling &amp; Peering Expansion:</strong> Drive initiatives to scale up localized peering and transit capacity with major eyeball networks across LATAM to support growing traffic demand, improve performance, and enhance latency.</li>
	<li><strong>Infrastructure Strategy &amp; Colocation Liaison:</strong> Collaborate with regional datacenter, colocation, and transport providers to ensure optimal infrastructure positioning, redundancy, and expansion readiness in key regional hubs.</li>
	<li><strong>Technical &amp; Operational Leadership:</strong> Act as an advisor to technical and operations teams on network health, traffic management, and routing optimization to safeguard network integrity and service reliability.</li>
	<li><strong>Regional Market Subject Matter Expertise:</strong> Serve as an internal subject matter expert for global network engineering, transport, and commercial teams, providing market context and operational guidance across diverse LATAM markets.</li>
	<li><strong>Cross-Functional Collaboration:</strong> Partner with internal commercial, channel, and engineering leadership to align infrastructure enablement with strategic regional goals.</li>
</ul>
<h3><strong>Must-Have Skills</strong></h3>
<ul>
	<li><strong>Leadership Experience:</strong> 15+ years of strategic leadership experience in telecommunications, interconnection/peering, datacenter colocation, or digital infrastructure across LATAM.</li>
	<li><strong>Partner Relationships:</strong> Extensive experience engaging with senior leadership at major telecommunications operators and service providers across key LATAM markets.</li>
	<li><strong>Technical SME:</strong> Strong understanding of IP routing (BGP), IXPs, DNS, traffic engineering, and content delivery networks.</li>
	<li><strong>Multilingual Capability:</strong> Professional fluency in English and Spanish is required; Portuguese fluency is preferred.</li>
	<li><strong>Collaboration &amp; Execution:</strong> Proven track record of driving cross-functional alignment and managing complex strategic initiatives across regional and global stakeholders.</li>
</ul>
<h3><strong>What We Offer</strong></h3>
