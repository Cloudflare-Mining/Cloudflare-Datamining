<h4><strong>About the role</strong></h4>
<p>The Global Vendor Operations Specialist is a hands-on operational role responsible for the day-to-day execution of vendor management activities across Cloudflare's global infrastructure operations. Working closely with the Global Vendor Manager, this role ensures vendors are properly onboarded, performing to agreed standards, and continuously improving through data-driven performance management.</p>
<p>This position serves as the primary operational liaison between Cloudflare and external service providers, translating strategic vendor relationships into tactical execution excellence.</p>
<h4><strong>Responsibilities</strong></h4>
<p><span style="text-decoration: underline;">Vendor Onboarding &amp; Integration</span></p>
<ul>
	<li>Guide new global vendors through the complete company setup process from contract signature to operational readiness</li>
	<li>Conduct vendor orientation sessions covering Cloudflare systems, communication protocols, escalation procedures, and daily operational workflows</li>
	<li>Coordinate collection and validation of required legal, tax, compliance, and security documentation before work authorization</li>
	<li>Create and maintain onboarding checklists, tracking templates, and vendor readiness dashboards</li>
	<li>Establish initial communication cadences and operational rhythms with new vendor teams</li>
	<li>Work with IT, Security, Procurement, and Legal teams to provision system access and complete required clearances</li>
</ul>
<p><span style="text-decoration: underline;">Metric Tracking &amp; Performance Scorecards</span></p>
<ul>
	<li>Build and maintain regular performance reports tracking vendor speed, quality, cost efficiency, and SLA compliance</li>
	<li>Monitor Service Level Agreements (SLAs) across all active vendors, flagging breaches and tracking resolution</li>
	<li>Develop vendor scorecards that provide clear, objective assessments of vendor performance</li>
	<li>Create executive-level dashboards summarizing vendor health across the global portfolio</li>
	<li>Establish data collection processes to ensure accurate, timely performance measurement</li>
	<li>Partner with vendors to align on measurement methodologies and data sources</li>
</ul>
<p><span style="text-decoration: underline;">Performance Improvement &amp; Issue Resolution</span></p>
<ul>
	<li>Conduct root cause analysis when vendors miss SLA targets or performance benchmarks</li>
	<li>Schedule and facilitate regular operational review meetings with vendor teams to discuss performance trends</li>
	<li>Track improvement plan progress and escalate persistent issues to the Global Vendor Manager</li>
	<li>Identify patterns across vendor performance issues and recommend systemic process improvements</li>
	<li>Document lessons learned from performance incidents to prevent recurrence</li>
</ul>
<p><span style="text-decoration: underline;">Process Optimization &amp; Automation</span></p>
<ul>
	<li>Simplify vendor operational guidelines, playbooks, and SOPs to improve clarity and compliance</li>
	<li>Identify opportunities to automate repetitive vendor management tasks using agentic AI tools (reporting, onboarding, compliance tracking)</li>
	<li>Partner with internal teams to implement tools that accelerate vendor onboarding and performance monitoring</li>
	<li>Create templates, forms, and workflows that reduce administrative overhead</li>
	<li>Maintain centralized vendor operations documentation and knowledge base</li>
	<li>Drive continuous improvement initiatives based on vendor and internal stakeholder feedback</li>
</ul>
<p><span style="text-decoration: underline;">Communication &amp; Stakeholder Management</span></p>
<ul>
	<li>Serve as the primary day-to-day point of contact for operational vendor inquiries</li>
	<li>Translate technical requirements from Infrastructure Operations teams into clear vendor work instructions</li>
	<li>Coordinate cross-functional stakeholders (Finance, Legal, Security, IT) during vendor lifecycle events</li>
	<li>Prepare and deliver regular status updates to leadership on vendor operational health</li>
	<li>Facilitate vendor business reviews and performance discussions</li>
	<li>Manage vendor communication channels (email, Slack, ticketing systems, etc.)</li>
</ul>
