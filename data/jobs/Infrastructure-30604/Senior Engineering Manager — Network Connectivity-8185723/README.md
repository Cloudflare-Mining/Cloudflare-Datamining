<p><strong>About the role</strong></p>
<p>The Egress team was formed to own a very important aspect of Cloudflare's <a href="https://www.cloudflare.com/connectivity-cloud/">Connectivity Cloud</a>: egress connectivity for our vast portfolio of products. The team build and operate cutting edge network software infrastructure used by a growing number of Cloudflare products to connect to any destination from Cloudflare's network. Among the products powered by this technology is <a href="https://developers.cloudflare.com/smart-shield/configuration/dedicated-egress-ips/how-it-works/egress-ips/">Dedicated Egress IP Connectivity</a>, WARP, Cloudflare One/Zero Trust, and Cloudflare's egress proxy for the<a href="https://blog.cloudflare.com/icloud-private-relay/"> iCloud Private Relay</a> product.</p>
<h4><strong>Responsibilities</strong></h4>
<p>As an Engineering Manager on the Egress team you will hire, mentor, and coach a distributed team of Software Engineers. You will play a key role in developing the team and shaping its culture and technical vision. This includes:</p>
<ul>
	<li>Working closely with Product Managers and other Engineering leaders to communicate the vision, prioritize the team's roadmap, and achieve cross-functional objectives.</li>
	<li>Understanding the technical implications of work across a distributed software network infrastructure to influence scope, work across teams, and help unblock projects: from data planes responsible for delivery of critical customer traffic to distributed control-plane services.</li>
	<li>Applying technical experience to evaluate tradeoffs, contribute to design decisions, and help drive resolution to customer-facing issues, while creating space for engineers to lead design, implementation, and delivery.</li>
	<li>Taking responsibility for the team's production services by organizing on-call rotations, handling customer escalations, and overseeing incident response and follow-up.</li>
	<li>Driving reliability and automation improvements across the platform</li>
	<li>Using tools and processes to convey project status and resolve blockages.</li>
</ul>
<p>You are a growth-oriented individual who enjoys being outside of your comfort zone. You are comfortable in a fast-paced but sensible work environment. You value curiosity and empathy and lead with these values.</p>
