<h3>About the Role</h3>
<p>The VoidZero team builds open-source tools that power the modern web. Our projects, like Vite, have pushed the frontiers of what is possible and are trusted by countless developers.</p>
<p>We are looking for someone to join the VoidZero team who is passionate about building community, education, and representing the VoidZero family of projects (Vite, Vitest, Rolldown, Oxc, Vite+). You'll help shape both how developers use our tools and how our tools are built for developers.</p>
<p>This role is a good fit for someone who identifies as a builder, teacher, mentor, and communicator. You're comfortable with both writing code and writing blog posts, and eager to meet developers in the places they best learn.</p>
<h3>What You'll Do</h3>
<ul>
	<li>Create content end to end. Teach developers how to use our products through writing, videos, talks, and code. This spans documentation, technical video content, live streaming, and rich tutorials.</li>
	<li>Represent the user during product discussions. Bring feedback, problems, and papercuts from the community back to our product teams to shape how our tools are built.</li>
	<li>Grow the community of developers using our products. Cultivate and establish relationships throughout the developer community and be a thoughtful, empathetic point of contact.</li>
	<li>Collaborate across the open-source ecosystem. Work with peers, partners, and maintainers to ship content showing how to integrate and extend the VoidZero projects.</li>
	<li>Partner across Cloudflare. Collaborate with product development, engineering, and marketing to ensure our content aligns with broader company and ecosystem goals.</li>
</ul>
