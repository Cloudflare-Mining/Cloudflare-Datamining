<h3 data-path-to-node="1"><strong data-path-to-node="1" data-index-in-node="0">Role Summary</strong></h3>
<p data-path-to-node="1">Replicate is now part of Cloudflare, and we are bringing its AI inference platform onto Cloudflare's global network and Workers AI. As a Senior Platform Engineer on the Replicate team, you will design, build, and operate the core distributed systems that accept, schedule, execute, and meter customer inference requests across Kubernetes clusters, multiple cloud providers, and heterogeneous GPU infrastructure. You will be a key technical driver in migrating Replicate’s models, workloads, and traffic onto Workers AI while maintaining high availability, low latency, and seamless backwards compatibility for existing customers. You will partner closely with engineers across Workers AI, core infrastructure, and product teams to solve hard problems in container orchestration, cold-start optimization, multi-tenant GPU scheduling, and high-throughput API routing. We are looking for an engineer who thrives in complex production environments, brings pragmatic architectural judgment, and elevates the engineering bar through strong code, thoughtful design reviews, and technical mentorship.</p>

<ul>
	<li><strong data-path-to-node="3,0,0" data-index-in-node="0">Architect and Build Core Platform Systems:</strong> Design, implement, and maintain the backend services powering Replicate's inference lifecycle—including public APIs, request queueing, workload scheduling, container runtime orchestration, and usage metering/billing.</li>
	<li><strong data-path-to-node="3,1,0" data-index-in-node="0">Drive the Workers AI Migration:</strong> Architect and execute the technical migration of Replicate models and live inference traffic to Workers AI, building translation layers, dual-routing/shadowing pipelines, API compatibility adapters, and graceful fallback mechanisms with zero customer disruption.</li>
	<li><strong data-path-to-node="3,2,0" data-index-in-node="0">Optimize Performance, Scale, and Cost:</strong> Profile and improve system throughput, cold-start latency, and GPU utilization across multi-cloud Kubernetes clusters and Cloudflare's edge infrastructure.</li>
	<li><strong data-path-to-node="3,3,0" data-index-in-node="0">Ensure Production Reliability:</strong> Participate in the team’s on-call rotation, lead triage and mitigation during live production incidents, author thorough post-incident reviews, and build automated remediation and observability tooling.</li>
	<li><strong data-path-to-node="3,4,0" data-index-in-node="0">Raise the Technical Bar:</strong> Author clear technical design documents (RFCs), conduct thorough code and architecture reviews, and mentor fellow engineers on distributed systems best practices and operational excellence.</li>
	<li><strong data-path-to-node="3,5,0" data-index-in-node="0">Cross-Team Technical Collaboration:</strong> Partner directly with Workers AI, networking, storage, and security engineering teams across Cloudflare to integrate Replicate’s platform primitives into Cloudflare’s broader AI stack.</li>
</ul>
