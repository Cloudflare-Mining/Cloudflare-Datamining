<h3>About the Role</h3>
<p>Cloudflare's developer platform lets developers build full-stack applications that run on a global network. Alongside Workers, developers rely on a growing set of data products: D1 for serverless SQLite, Durable Objects for stateful coordination, KV for global key-value storage, R2 for object storage, and Hyperdrive for connecting to existing databases.</p>
<p>This role will be leading a new database product from its earliest days. You will define what the product is, who it is for, and why developers should choose it over the databases they already know. Then you will get it to first customers and see it through to general availability.</p>
<p>Your mission in this role is to lead the project of building a database designed for agent-driven development workflows, getting both the core tradeoffs and the fine-grained details right.</p>
<h3>What You'll Do</h3>
<ul>
	<li>Run customer discovery to find and sharpen the problem the product should solve.</li>
	<li>Define the product, its target users, and its positioning against established databases.</li>
	<li>Work with engineering on core design tradeoffs across consistency, latency, scale, durability, and cost to serve.</li>
	<li>Recruit design partners and run a private beta that produces honest, usable feedback.</li>
	<li>Set success metrics and use them to decide what to build, change, or cut.</li>
	<li>Own pricing and packaging, grounded in customer value and unit economics.</li>
	<li>Drive the product from first prototype through beta, launch, and GA.</li>
	<li>Make sure it fits naturally with Workers, Wrangler, and Cloudflare's existing data products.</li>
	<li>Partner cross-functionally with Product Marketing, Developer Relations, Sales, and Customer Support teams.</li>
	<li>Represent Cloudflare externally at conferences, community events, customer meetings, and developer-focused initiatives.</li>
</ul>
<h3>Examples of Desirable Skills, Knowledge, and Experience</h3>
<ul>
	<li>Proven experience as an EM, PM or founder. You've led teams to ship before and can point to impact.</li>
	<li>Experience taking a product from 0 to 1. You are comfortable with ambiguity and can turn an open problem into a clear plan.</li>
	<li>Real experience building on databases. You have modeled data, written queries, and run SQL or NoSQL databases in production.</li>
	<li>Solid grasp of distributed-systems tradeoffs. You can reason through replication, consistency models, and failure modes with engineers.</li>
	<li>Comfort with pricing and unit economics for infrastructure. You know what a workload costs to serve and how that shapes a price.</li>
	<li>Deep empathy for developers and how they choose and adopt new platforms. You understand what it's like to bet your application on a database someone else built.</li>
	<li>Hands-on ability to write code and use AI and the Internet to learn new things fast.</li>
	<li>Exceptional communication and collaboration skills. You can translate between engineering domains and align diverse teams on goals.</li>
	<li>Passion for design details and polish. You raise the bar for sweating the details.</li>
	<li>External presentation skills. You can walk into a room of developers and build trust and credibility.</li>
</ul>
