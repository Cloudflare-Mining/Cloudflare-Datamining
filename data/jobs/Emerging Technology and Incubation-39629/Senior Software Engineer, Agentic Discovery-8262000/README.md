		<h3>About the Department</h3>
		<p>Emerging Technologies &amp; Incubation (ETI) is where new and bold products are built and released within Cloudflare. Rather than being constrained by the structures which make Cloudflare a hugely successful business, we are able to leverage them to deliver entirely new tools and products to our customers. 		<h3>About the Team</h3>
		<p>We build the products that enable the next era of the Internet: making domains <strong>Readable</strong>, <strong>Discoverable</strong>, and <strong>Callable</strong> for autonomous AI agents.</p>
		<p>Here at Cloudflare, agentic discovery is a core part of our vision for the <a href="https://blog.cloudflare.com/the-agentic-internet/">Agentic Internet</a>. As AI agents change how humans and machines find, evaluate, and act on information, businesses need tools to control how they are represented and reached. We build capabilities that enable website owners to prepare their sites for agent interaction, test and observe how agents read their content, establish open interaction standards (like WebMCP and MCP), and create entirely new channels to reach agentic audiences.</p>
		<p>We are a cross functional group that communicates and collaborates regularly. We celebrate our achievements, support each other when we make mistakes, and hold each other and our work to a high standard.</p>
		<h3>About the Role</h3>
		<p>We are looking for a Senior Software Engineer to serve as a technical anchor and become a subject matter expert on our Agentic Discovery team. In this role, you will define the architecture, core protocols, and multi-team standards powering the next generation of agent-web interactions.</p>
		<p>As a senior technical leader, you will shape product roadmaps, mentor engineers across Cloudflare, and write technical specifications that drive execution across multiple teams. You will drive engineering excellence across edge infrastructure, data pipelines, protocol layers, and developer tooling, ensuring systems are resilient and complexity is reduced for your peers.</p>

		<ul>
