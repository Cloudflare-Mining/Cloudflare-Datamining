<h1>Engineering Manager, Agentic Discovery</h1>
<h2>About the Department</h2>
<p>Emerging Technologies &amp; Incubation (ETI) is where new and bold products are built and released within Cloudflare. Rather than being constrained by the structures which make Cloudflare a hugely successful business, we are able to leverage them to deliver entirely new tools and products to our customers. Cloudflare’s edge and network make it possible to solve problems at massive scale and efficiency which would be impossible for almost any other organization.</p>
<h2>About the Team</h2>
<p>We build the products that enable the next era of the Internet: making domains <strong>Readable</strong>, <strong>Discoverable</strong>, and <strong>Callable</strong> for autonomous AI agents.</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p>Here at Cloudflare, agentic discovery is a core part of our vision for the <a href="https://blog.cloudflare.com/the-agentic-internet/">Agentic Internet</a>. As AI agents change how humans and machines find, evaluate, and act on information, businesses need tools to control how they are represented and reached. We build capabilities that enable website owners to prepare their sites for agent interaction, test and observe how agents read their content, establish open interaction standards (like WebMCP), and create entirely new channels to reach agentic audiences.</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p>We are a tight-knit group that communicates and collaborates regularly. We celebrate our achievements, support each other when we make mistakes, and hold each other and our work to a high standard.</p>
<h2>About the Role</h2>
<p>We are looking for an Engineering Manager to build and lead the engineering team across our Agentic Discovery portfolio.</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p>You will be responsible for the people, execution, and engineering quality behind customer-facing products that help businesses manage how AI agents understand, represent, and interact with their digital properties. You will partner closely with Product Management and engineering teams across Cloudflare to shape priorities around Answer Engine Optimization (AEO), content ingestion/indexing, agent observation and testing tools, and open tool-execution standards (such as WebMCP and Generative UI).</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p>This is a management role for someone who is deeply technically credible. You will not be expected to write most of the code, but you should be able to understand the systems your engineers operate, challenge architectural decisions around distributed systems and web-scale data pipelines, ask the right questions when something is going wrong, and step into a technical problem when the situation calls for it.</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p>At the same time, we are looking for a manager, not a tech lead with direct reports. Your success will be measured by the quality and output of the team you build, the engineers you develop, and the systems and products they deliver.</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p>This role may require flexibility to be on-call outside of standard working hours to address technical issues as needed.</p>
<h2>What you'll do</h2>
<ul>
	<li>Manage, coach, and grow engineers working across customer-facing Agentic Discovery products, AEO tooling, and agent-web interaction standards.</li>
	<li>Own execution by establishing clear priorities, creating predictable delivery, and giving engineers the context and autonomy to own meaningful areas of the product.</li>
	<li>Lead the development of tools enabling Cloudflare customers to make their content <strong>Readable</strong> (structured content transformation), <strong>Discoverable</strong> (indexing, directories, and AEO), and <strong>Callable</strong> (WebMCP, APIs, Generative UI) for autonomous agents.</li>
	<li>Build tools for customers to test, observe, measure, and control how AI agents find and interact with their websites and applications.</li>
	<li>Partner closely with Product Management to understand customer problems, shape the roadmap, and turn broad opportunities into clear engineering outcomes.</li>
	<li>Identify how Cloudflare's global network and edge signals can enable unique products that help businesses reach trusted agentic audiences at scale.</li>
	<li>Balance near-term product delivery with thoughtful technical investments that allow promising ideas to grow.</li>
	<li>Own the operational health of the systems in your area, including observability, incident response, data quality, and long-term reliability.</li>
	<li>Develop technical leaders and create space for engineers to lead architecture and implementation.</li>
</ul>
<h2>Responsibilities</h2>
<p>We want you to love it here. This role is a great fit for you if:</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<ul>
	<li>You have led and developed a high-performing engineering team(s) and can point to what that team shipped and how the people on it grew.</li>
	<li>You are technically credible with experienced engineers. You can review a design, understand the trade-offs in distributed systems or web-scale data pipelines, and recognize when a technical problem needs deeper investigation.</li>
	<li>You are comfortable managing a team working across several related technical areas (such as search/indexing, protocol standards, and UI engineering) and can create clarity without requiring every problem to fit neatly into a single box.</li>
