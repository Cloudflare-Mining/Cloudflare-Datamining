	<h3>About Us</h3>
	<p>At Cloudflare, we are on a mission to help build a better Internet. Today the company runs one of the world’s largest networks that powers millions of websites and other Internet properties for customers ranging from individual bloggers to SMBs to Fortune 500 companies. Cloudflare protects and accelerates any Internet application online without adding hardware, installing software, or changing a line of code. Internet properties powered by Cloudflare all have web traffic routed through its intelligent global network, which gets smarter with every request. As a result, they see significant improvement in performance and a decrease in spam and other attacks. Cloudflare was named to Entrepreneur Magazine’s Top Company Cultures list and ranked among the World’s Most Innovative Companies by Fast Company.</p>

We value candidates who have the instinct to spot a "normalized" problem and the AI-native curiosity to create a solution using the latest tools. Our culture is built on iteration, leveraging AI to ship faster today to make it better tomorrow, while ensuring that every improvement, no matter how small, is shared across the team to lift everyone up. If you’re the type of person who values curiosity over bureaucracy, and that AI is a partner in solving tough problems to keep the Internet moving forward, you’ll fit right in.</p>

<h2>About the Department</h2>
<p>Cloudflare helps build a better Internet and the Emerging Technology and Incubation (ETI) team invents, launches, and brings to market new technologies built on Cloudflare's network. ETI makes dozens of bets on the future of the Internet and takes the ones that work to market.</p>
<p>ETI started in 2016 and incubated several of Cloudflare's major product lines, including Zero Trust and the Developer Platform. Today ETI is focused on the agentic Internet: how AI agents find, read, and pay for content and services, and how creators and companies get paid as agents become a larger share of Internet traffic.</p>
<p>Every bet in ETI follows the same loop: test an idea, build the smallest version that tells us something, get real feedback, iterate, and ship. The team moves quickly and works closely with teams across Cloudflare.</p>
<h2>About the Role</h2>
<p>You will be Chief of Staff to the head of ETI, working alongside the product managers and engineering leaders on each bet. You will help the head of ETI stay high level while you handle much of the execution. You will be read in on almost everything in the portfolio, so discretion matters. Over time, you will represent them in meetings.</p>
<p><strong>Operations and Strategic Initiatives: </strong>Lead the strategic initiatives that start in ETI or rely on the ETI team. These can include acquisition due diligence and integration, partnerships that extend Cloudflare's technology to new customers, and anything else the portfolio needs. Build the tooling and workflows ETI is missing, thinking through the dependencies.</p>
<p><strong>Team and Project Management: </strong>Run the operating rhythm with the teams. Drive decisions and plans to closure, follow through on what is owed, and prep and close out the big moments such as planning cycles, onsites, and key forums. The teams will trust you to help set priorities across the group and to own new projects or dependencies. You will partner with the executive assistant, who handles booking, expenses, and logistics, while you help prioritize the calendar.</p>
<p><strong>Research and Analysis: </strong>Get into the details with the teams: research and analysis, solving problems, and producing the docs and materials the head of ETI needs while keeping them human. You will be a real sounding board, with useful commentary and feedback.</p>
<p><strong>Communications: </strong>Help ETI tell its story, internally and, with the Communications team, externally. Own the presentations and materials ETI shares, including retrospectives and celebrations, and help the product, engineering, and marketing teams get the inputs they need to tell specific stories.</p>
