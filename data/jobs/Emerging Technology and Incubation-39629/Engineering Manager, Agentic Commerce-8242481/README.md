<h2>About the Team</h2>
<p>We build the infrastructure that makes the web <strong>Payable</strong>, powering the future of machine-to-machine commerce.</p>
<p>Here at Cloudflare, agentic commerce is a core part of our vision for the <a href="https://blog.cloudflare.com/the-agentic-internet/">Agentic Internet</a>. As AI agents increasingly navigate, consume, and act on the web, standard payment and authorization models built for humans are breaking down. We are building tools that help make the new internet <strong>payable</strong> by giving our users the ability to take control over how AI agents pay for resources across the Internet.</p>
<p>Building these capabilities requires solving problems across high-throughput web infrastructure, low-latency transaction processing, digital wallets, and emerging agentic payment protocols. We bridge mainstream fiat commerce rails with decentralized protocols, enabling micro-payments at Internet scale. We’re looking for someone excited to deliver products so domain owners can control this experience, while identifying the problems Cloudflare is uniquely positioned to solve next.</p>
<p>We are a tight-knit group that communicates and collaborates regularly. We celebrate our achievements, support each other when we make mistakes, and hold each other and our work to a high standard.</p>
<h2>About the role</h2>
<p>We are looking for an Engineering Manager to build and lead the engineering team across Agentic Commerce.</p>
<p>You will be responsible for the people, execution, and engineering quality behind products that power machine-to-machine payments and monetization, such as our <a href="https://blog.cloudflare.com/monetization-gateway/">Monetization Gateway</a>. You will partner closely with Product Management and engineering teams across Cloudflare to deliver current priorities and shape future opportunities. This is a management role for someone who is deeply technically credible. You will not be expected to write most of the code, but you should be able to understand the systems your engineers operate, challenge architectural decisions around payment rails and cryptographic auth, ask the right questions when something is going wrong, and step into a technical problem when the situation calls for it.</p>
<p>At the same time, we are looking for a manager, not a tech lead with direct reports. Your success will be measured by the quality and output of the team you build, the engineers you develop, and the systems and products they deliver.</p>
<p>This role may require flexibility to be on-call outside of standard working hours to address technical issues as needed.</p>
<h2>What you'll do</h2>
<ul>
	<li>Manage, coach, and grow engineers working across high-throughput payment pipelines, agent authentication, and web-scale commerce architecture.</li>
	<li>Own execution by establishing clear priorities, creating predictable delivery, and giving engineers the context and autonomy to own meaningful areas of the product.</li>
	<li>Lead the development of agentic payment systems leveraging emerging open standards (x402, Web Bot Auth, ACP, UCP) to enable low-latency, machine-to-machine micropayments.</li>
	<li>Lead the engineering behind usage metering, pricing definition engines, outcome verification, and revenue attribution models tailored for agentic workflows.</li>
	<li>Build capabilities that give businesses granular visibility and control over their agent-generated revenue streams.</li>
	<li>Partner closely with Product Management to understand customer problems, shape the roadmap, and turn broad opportunities into clear engineering outcomes.</li>
	<li>Bridge mainstream and decentralized tech, interfacing with blockchain teams while keeping execution focused on mainstream, high-throughput web and fiat commerce rails.</li>
	<li>Balance near-term product delivery with thoughtful technical investments that allow promising ideas to grow.</li>
	<li>Own the operational health of the systems in your area, including observability, incident response, cryptographic security, and long-term reliability.</li>
	<li>Develop technical leaders and create space for engineers to lead architecture and implementation.</li>
</ul>
