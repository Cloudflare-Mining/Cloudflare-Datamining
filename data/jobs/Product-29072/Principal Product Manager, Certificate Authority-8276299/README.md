<h3 id="PrincipalProductManager,CertificateAuthority-AbouttheRole">About the Role</h3>
<p>TLS is the connective tissue of the Internet, and Cloudflare terminates a large fraction of it. Cloudflare sits in front of more than 20 percent of global Internet request traffic and terminates TLS for millions of domains. Every day, our network handles certificate operations across products like Universal SSL, SSL for SaaS, Origin CA, Cloudflare Access, and Cloudflare Tunnel.</p>
<p>That scale has taught us how the WebPKI ecosystem works from the consuming side.&nbsp; As certificate lifetimes shorten, agentic activity increases, and post-quantum certificates move toward production, the number of certificates the Internet depends on will grow dramatically.&nbsp;</p>
<p>Cloudflare is building a certificate authority for the whole Internet: a public, redundant, transparent CA that provides another trusted source of automated certificates and helps make the encrypted web more resilient. We intend to be ACME-first, automation-first, and transparent by design. We also intend to lead where the ecosystem is going, including post-quantum certificates and Merkle Tree Certificates, while continuing to support the classic WebPKI customers rely on today.</p>
<p>As Principal Product Manager for Cloudflare's Certificate Authority business, you will define the product strategy, roadmap, customer experience, and commercial model for this new venture. You will work across product, engineering, security, compliance, legal, operations, sales, marketing, and the WebPKI ecosystem to turn Cloudflare's CA from a major technical commitment into a product customers trust and adopt.</p>
<p>This is an early-days role on a growing team. It is not a role for someone who only manages backlog. The right person will be comfortable with high-consequence infrastructure, deep customer discovery, ecosystem trust, compliance scrutiny, and zero-to-one product building. The decisions you make in the first year will shape how Cloudflare participates in the certificate authority market for years to come.</p>
<h3 id="PrincipalProductManager,CertificateAuthority-Responsibilities">Responsibilities</h3>
<ul>
	<li><strong>Define the product strategy for Cloudflare's CA business</strong>&nbsp;across publicly trusted certificates, certificate lifecycle automation, ACME issuance, renewal workflows, revocation, Certificate Transparency, customer controls, developer experience, and integrations with Cloudflare's broader platform.</li>
	<li><strong>Build for the whole Internet</strong>&nbsp;by understanding the needs of developers, site owners, platform teams, SaaS providers, enterprise security teams, infrastructure operators, and partners who depend on certificates for availability, security, and compliance.</li>
	<li><strong>Make resilience a product principle.</strong>&nbsp;Help design product requirements that fail small, reduce customer blast radius, support automated recovery, and make certificate replacement fast enough to handle compliance issues, revocation events, or ecosystem incidents.</li>
	<li><strong>Drive an ACME-first customer experience</strong>&nbsp;that makes it easy for users of existing free or automated CAs to adopt Cloudflare with minimal tooling changes.</li>
	<li><strong>Partner closely with engineering</strong>&nbsp;on systems that manage certificate lifecycles, automate issuance and renewal, integrate with HSMs, protect keys, support auditability, and operate within strict reliability and correctness requirements.</li>
	<li><strong>Partner with compliance, legal, security, and operations</strong>&nbsp;to ensure product decisions align with CA/Browser Forum Baseline Requirements, WebTrust audits, root store program policies, Certificate Policy and Certification Practice Statement commitments, and public incident response expectations.</li>
	<li><strong>Own go-to-market strategy</strong>&nbsp;including packaging, pricing, launch sequencing, competitive positioning, customer segmentation, sales enablement, developer messaging, and adoption metrics.</li>
	<li><strong>Represent the customer and the ecosystem</strong>&nbsp;in product decisions where trust, transparency, uptime, security, and commercial adoption must all be balanced.</li>
	<li><strong>Work across Cloudflare product teams</strong>&nbsp;including SSL/TLS, Application Security, Zero Trust, Workers, Registrar, DNS, Load Balancing, SaaS products, and infrastructure teams that consume or expose certificate services.</li>
	<li><strong>Help make Cloudflare Customer Zero</strong>&nbsp;by ensuring our own usage of the CA exercises the product at Cloudflare scale and informs the roadmap before customers depend on it.</li>
</ul>
