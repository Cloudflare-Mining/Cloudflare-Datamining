<h4><strong>About the Department</strong></h4>
<p>The Product Management team at Cloudflare steers the strategy, roadmap, and execution that powers a faster, more secure internet. As part of the Zero Trust Security organization, our Email Security team is redefining how the enterprise's most trusted — and most targeted — communication channel gets protected, evolving a platform already trusted by Fortune 500 and Global 2000 organizations into a fast, secure pillar natively integrated with Cloudflare's Zero Trust stack.</p>
<h4><strong>About the role</strong></h4>
<p>Email remains the most trusted, yet most targeted, communication channel in the enterprise. At Cloudflare, we have a proven email security solution which is trusted by Fortune 500 and Global 2000 organizations. We’re looking for an experienced, technical product manager to continue to differentiate our solution in the market, make difficult investment decisions, and own delivering an enterprise-grade email security platform that is fast, secure, and natively integrated with Cloudflare's Zero Trust stack.</p>
<p>We are seeking an autonomous, determined, and pragmatic individual who possesses strong conviction, humility, and a decisive bias toward action.</p>
<h4>What you'll do</h4>
<p>In this role, you’ll be responsible for defining how some of the world's most sophisticated organizations protect their communication, leading the industry's shift away from legacy "boxes" toward an AI-first, cloud-native, and identity-aware security posture.&nbsp;</p>
<p>Key responsibilities involve continuously investing in threat intelligence, sustaining current security detection capabilities, rapidly developing features, and expanding into new markets.</p>
<h4><strong>Responsibilities</strong></h4>
<ul>
	<li><strong>Own Email Security.</strong> Architect and own the strategic roadmap for our Email Security solution, ensuring Cloudflare remains the definitive answer to evolving threats like Business Email Compromise (BEC) and advanced phishing.</li>
	<li><strong>Shape Intelligence and Detections.</strong> Work alongside our Threat Intelligence and Detections teams to improve how customers consume, enforce, and understand Cloudflare One. Influence product direction, connect customer needs across the enterprise and own selected initiatives when dedicated leadership will accelerate an important outcome.</li>
	<li><strong>Support Zero Trust Security.</strong> Bolster the seamless integration of Email Security into the broader Cloudflare stack, architecting a "better together" experience that bridges Gateway, CASB, and DLP into a single, powerhouse defense layer.</li>
	<li><strong>Talk to Customers</strong>. Build trust with engineering and sales by rigorously documenting first-hand experiences with internal and external customers.&nbsp;</li>
	<li><strong>Drive new business.</strong> Act as the strategic engine for growth, building the features and competitive differentiators needed to displace legacy incumbents and win the hearts and minds of Global 2000 CISOs.</li>
	<li><strong>Evangelize the Platform.</strong> Use the product daily and serve as a technical and strategic leader, representing the Email Security vision to internal stakeholders, the sales field, and the broader security community.</li>
</ul>
