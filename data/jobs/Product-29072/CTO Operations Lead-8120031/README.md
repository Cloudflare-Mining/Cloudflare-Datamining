<p>The Office of the CTO is the strategic engine for Cloudflare’s technology vision. <h3><strong>What You'll Do</strong></h3>
<p>As the CTO Operations Lead you will act as a strategic force multiplier for the Chief Technology Officer. Working alongside the CTO Chief of Staff you will help manage priorities, ensuring the CTO’s time is aligned with Cloudflare’s most critical technical and business goals. This role requires a blend of operational rigor, strategic thinking, and the ability to navigate complex cross-functional landscapes.</p>
<p><strong>Key Responsibilities:</strong></p>
<ul>
	<li><strong>Drive Operational Excellence:</strong> Orchestrate the rhythm of the business for the CTO department. This includes ensuring the department remains compliant with all training and regulatory obligations.</li>
	<li><strong>Strategic Briefing &amp; Preparation:</strong> Research and synthesize context for the CTO ahead of external engagements and key customer calls. You will ensure the CTO is equipped with actionable insights, background data, and strategic talking points to maximize the impact of every interaction.</li>
	<li><strong>Actionable Follow-Through:</strong> Attend high-priority internal and external calls to capture critical context. You will own the lifecycle of action items, driving accountability across teams and ensuring that decisions made in meetings translate into tangible results.</li>
	<li><strong>Narrative Construction:</strong> Partner with the CTO to craft compelling presentations and written materials for internal all-hands, board meetings, and external keynotes. You will help translate complex technical concepts into clear, impactful narratives.</li>
	<li><strong>Project Leadership:</strong> Lead special projects and initiatives within the Office of the CTO, often navigating ambiguity to define scope, stakeholders, and success metrics.<br><br></li>
</ul>
