<p><strong>About the Role</strong></p>
<p>Cloudflare is looking for a PR Specialist, APAC + Japan to assist the head of PR, Asia Pacific, in scaling our PR program internally and externally. In this role you’ll get to work with a wide range of Cloudflare executives across technical, legal/policy, marketing, and regional teams. The ideal candidate should have at least 3-5 years of regional tech PR experience, supporting and executing PR programs in Southeast Asia/Asia Pacific. You’ll be a key member of our award-winning in-house communications team, and will work with a wide range of executives and teams to support execution, develop messaging, and report results from concept to completion. You'll also help extend our story beyond earned media across APAC + Japan, using social listening to spot conversations and trends in the region, and posting approved content to amplify Cloudflare news and coverage on social channels.</p>

<ul>

	<li>Help manage day-to-day execution for APAC PR programs&nbsp;</li>
	<li>Maintain strong media relationships across all types of media outlets, develop story ideas, and manage stories</li>
	<li>Organize media briefings, interviews, and PR-focused events</li>

	<li>Monitor social and online conversations about Cloudflare, competitors, and key industry topics across APAC + Japan, flagging trends, newsjacking opportunities, and emerging issues to the Sr. PR Director, APAC + Japan</li>
	<li>Track sentiment and share of voice in APAC + Japan around announcements, campaigns, and Internet events, and turn the findings into regular reports and insights for the comms team</li>
</ul>
