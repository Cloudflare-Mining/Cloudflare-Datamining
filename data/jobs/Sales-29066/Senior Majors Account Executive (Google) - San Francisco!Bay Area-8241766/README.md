<h2>About the Role</h2>
<p>We are seeking an experienced and strategic Senior Majors Account Executive to join our Enterprise/Field Sales team. In this pivotal role, you will lead Cloudflare’s strategic engagement with Google, securing and expanding platform partnerships across this key account. The ideal candidate brings strong sales leadership, a deep understanding of enterprise architecture (business, data, applications, technology), and the ability to articulate how Cloudflare integrates into modern cloud operating models.</p>
<p>As a Senior Majors Account Executive, you will drive predictable sales performance, foster healthy pipeline growth, and deliver on revenue objectives. You will act as a trusted advisor, educating customers and partners on Cloudflare’s evolving product ecosystem and security capabilities. This role involves leveraging executive sponsorship programs, partnering with senior leaders (including CIOs, CISOs, and CTOs), and collaborating with cross-functional teams to navigate and successfully close complex customer opportunities.</p>
