<p>About the role:&nbsp;</p>
<p>Cloudflare is looking for an <strong>APJC Benefits Leader and Program Manager</strong> to join our global Total Rewards team. Reporting to the Senior Director, Head of Global Benefits, you will serve as a subject matter expert and thought leader for country program benefits in our APJC region, including Singapore, India, Australia, Japan, South Korea, China, and Malaysia.</p>
<p><strong>What you’ll do:</strong></p>
<ul>
	<li>Manage and lead existing benefit programs (e.g., health, dental, vision, disability, life insurance, workers comp, travel and accident, retirement and other plans) - including new plan implementation, end-to-end contracting, etc. - while aligning regional benefits strategy to the department's global direction.</li>
	<li>Work cross-functionally to introduce, maintain and improve end-to-end benefits management processes in the region and to harmonize policies and processes globally, where applicable.</li>
	<li>Ensure that all benefits programs are designed to resonate to hire and attract talent; reflect legal and business requirements; are competitive with our known benchmarking peers, and can be sourced locally (when needed).</li>
	<li>Lead the design of new benefit programs/proposals that align to Cloudflare’s Global Benefits Strategy, new country expansion activities, market practice, benchmarking, and local regulatory/compliance requirements.&nbsp;</li>
	<li>Manage and monitor regional benefit program costs; develop and manage annual renewals, including associated annual regional budget and cost impact analysis; ensure programs evolve&nbsp; in line with headcount increases, market trends, employee needs and ROI.</li>
	<li>Review proposals with internal stakeholders to receive input and feedback on recommendations, including the taxability of proposed benefit programs.</li>
	<li>Collaborate with the Global Head of Benefits and US and EMEA Benefit Leaders to implement effective structure for the department's new global benefits management (GBM) broker and consultant model.</li>
	<li>Work closely with global benefits broker/consultant partners to perform regular benchmarking and analysis of current programs and comparison to firm's tech peers; monitor, measure, and evaluate performance of regional broker/consultant.</li>
	<li>Oversee the management of local/region vendor relationships; collaborate with APJC People Operations Leader to develop accountability measures to monitor vendor and program operation performance in the region to deliver a positive employee experience and to ensure compliance with local/regional regulations.</li>
	<li>Work with Global Time Away practice leader and Global Time Away consultant to help coordinate and set leave of absence policies and processes.</li>
	<li>Provide consultative support and act as a trusted advisor to Total Rewards, People Operations, P&amp;P, and Business leaders by effectively offering data-driven insights to inform future analysis and decisions on regional benefit initiatives.&nbsp;</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Examples of desirable skills, knowledge and experience:</strong></p>
<ul>
	<li>Minimum of 12+ years of <strong>direct benefit program management experience </strong>in an APJC&nbsp; multi-country or global environment with multinational employers (including team management responsibilities)</li>
	<li>Proven ability to operate at a strategic level with adaptability to dive deep and pivot tactically as required to ensure effective delivery performance.</li>
	<li>Demonstrated expertise working within global benefits strategy development and program administration and governance.</li>
	<li>Detailed knowledge of employee benefit plan design in APJC, including healthcare and pension schemes along with associated complexities of plan administration, associated payroll processes, country-level regulatory requirements, and benefits program taxability.</li>
	<li>Successful record of building internal and strategic vendor partnerships globally, driving program efficiency along with measuring and maintaining high levels of customer satisfaction.</li>
	<li>Demonstrated bias for action, program accountability, and extreme ownership; with the ability to work in a fast-paced environment with a high degree of autonomy through ambiguous phases of expansion and project development.</li>
	<li>Excellent project management and organizational skills, with great attention to detail and the ability to navigate across business units and critical business priorities.</li>
	<li>Possess sound personal judgment and global business acumen, with the ability to influence business leaders and to interface with all levels of the organization.</li>
	<li>Superb verbal and written communication skills, including the ability to present and discuss complex information to a diverse audience in a way that persuades others and enhances understanding.</li>
	<li>Proven abilities in vendor management, vendor accountability, and establishing effective partnerships with external service providers to ensure quality service is delivered to employees.</li>
	<li>Highly proficient in MS Excel, PowerPoint, Word and Google Suite (Sheets, Docs, Slides).</li>
	<li>Strong working knowledge of HR systems (i. e. Workday) and global payroll systems (i.e., Global ADP).</li>
</ul>
