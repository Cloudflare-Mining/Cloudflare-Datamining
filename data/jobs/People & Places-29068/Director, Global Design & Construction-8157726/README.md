<h3><strong>About the Role</strong></h3>
<p>We are looking for an innovative, tech-forward <strong>Director of Global Design &amp; Construction</strong> to lead our physical environment strategy into its next evolution. This role demands a unique combination of high-level strategic vision, a passion for experimentation, creativity, and a relentless commitment to world-class physical execution.</p>
<p>First and foremost, you are a builder of impactful, high-performance spaces. You will be responsible for leading the end-to-end design and construction of workspaces that serve as the tangible, physical manifestation of the local Cloudflare culture.</p>
<p>At the same time, you are stepping into uncharted territory. To keep Cloudflare at the absolute forefront of our industry, we are up-leveling how we deliver projects by integrating an AI-first, data-driven approach. We are looking for a visionary leader who can think outside the box to identify and implement how AI can automate workflows, optimize budgets, and improve data-driven design decisions.</p>
<p>While automation and AI are the engines of efficiency in this role, the <strong>human element</strong> is its heart. Your creativity, physical hospitality, and human judgment will guide every project, ensuring our global spaces continuously evolve alongside our teams.</p>
<h3><strong>What You’ll Do</strong></h3>
<ul>
	<li><strong>Physical Execution &amp; Cultural Identity:</strong> Lead the physical realization of Cloudflare’s global offices. Direct architects, general contractors, and artists to design and build spaces that authentically capture the vibe and culture of each local hub, ensuring every environment feels uniquely Cloudflare, inspiring, and functional.</li>
	<li><strong>Conceptualize and execute the AI Workflow:</strong> Think outside the box to architect how AI and automation can revolutionize legacy design and construction phases. Map out and execute opportunities to eliminate manual bottlenecks across the entire project lifecycle - including vendor onboarding/benchmarking, contracting, cost estimation, budget/project tracking, and risk identification - by implementing automated review pipelines and data-driven quality controls.</li>
	<li><strong>Drive Spatial Experimentation:</strong> Treat our workplaces as living laboratories. Conceptualize and implement pioneering internal qualitative and quantitative data-collection methods - such as tracking real-time utilization patterns and capturing continuous employee feedback loops - to architect data-driven proposals that evolve our global footprints based on your findings.</li>
	<li><strong>Evolve the Design Framework:</strong> Use data insights and post-occupancy metrics to constantly challenge existing design assumptions. Lead formal change management efforts to iterate on our physical footprints and champion a generative design-supported “kit of parts” for global serviced offices, ensuring they fluidly adapt as work styles and distributed team needs change.</li>
	<li><strong>Predictive Capital &amp; Site Strategy:</strong> Collaborate with Real Estate and FP&amp;A teams to direct predictive modeling concepts for site selection, due diligence, project cash flows, long-term financial reporting, and financial risk mitigation, steering the department toward highly optimized capital allocation.</li>
	<li><strong>CapEx Budget Ownership &amp; Financial Governance:</strong> Own and manage the global capital expenditure (CapEx) budget, delivering accurate multi-year financial forecasting, cost-variance reporting, and executive-level capital reporting.</li>
	<li><strong>Executive Cross-Functional Leadership:</strong> Serve as the strategic bridge and primary liaison between internal executive business customers and external delivery partners (including architects, GCs, and furniture dealers). Ensure Physical Security, IT, Legal, and Workplace leadership are deeply aligned under a unified, technology-supported project pipeline and data layer that prioritizes the employee experience.</li>
	<li><strong>Lifecycle Handover &amp; Commissioning:</strong> Establish rigorous commissioning protocols to ensure flawless handovers from construction completion to local Workplace Operations&nbsp; teams.</li>
	<li><strong>Global Pipeline Governance &amp; Compliance:</strong> Oversee a global project pipeline across all international hubs and time zones, seamlessly balancing highly creative local refreshes with complex, large-scale 100,000+ square foot tenant improvement projects. Ensure all construction activities and global workflows are in strict compliance with local laws while maintaining a focus on sustainable, high-performance building standards.</li>
</ul>
