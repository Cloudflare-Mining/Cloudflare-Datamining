<h2>About the Role</h2>
<p>The Places Team at Cloudflare is seeking an experienced, operationally rigorous Workplace Operations Manager to lead daily onsite workplace operations for our Singapore office. In this role, you will serve as the hands-on operational site lead and brand ambassador, driving workplace standards, executing regional directives, and delivering a consistent, high-touch employee and guest experience across our APAC hub.</p>
<p>Reporting directly to the Regional Workplace Operations Manager – APAC, you will translate regional strategy into efficient site execution. You will partner with regional leadership, cross-functional teams (IT, Physical Security, People Operations, Legal), and local service partners to maintain a highly functional, welcoming workplace. Balancing local site ownership with regional alignment, you bring practical expertise in facilities coordination, end-to-end vendor lifecycle management (including RFPs and contract reviews), and budget tracking, paired with a hospitality mindset that reflects Cloudflare’s values and culture.</p>
<h2>Responsibilities</h2>
<ul>
	<li><strong>Lead Onsite Hub Operations &amp; Space Readiness:</strong> Direct daily workplace operations across the Singapore site—overseeing front-of-house services, shipping/receiving, routine building maintenance, and space readiness to maintain an organized, welcoming workplace.</li>
	<li><strong>Vendor Management, RFPs &amp; Contract Renewals:</strong> Manage the full vendor lifecycle across janitorial, catering, and facilities services. Build collaborative vendor relationships, lead local RFP processes, track SLA performance, and conduct service reviews to drive contract renewals.</li>
	<li><strong>Site Budget Tracking &amp; Expense Management:</strong> Monitor and track the local operating expense (OpEx) budget in partnership with APAC leadership. Review invoices, manage POs, track monthly spend, and identify cost efficiencies.</li>
	<li><strong>Workplace Experience &amp; Programs:</strong> Champion workplace culture and hybrid collaboration by delivering regional workplace programs, high-touch onsite initiatives, and day-to-day food, beverage, and amenity offerings.</li>
	<li><strong>Site Communications &amp; Operational Insights:</strong> Act as the primary operational contact for Singapore site leadership and staff. Monitor and report workplace metrics (occupancy, space utilization, ticketing trends) to inform space planning and escalate site risks.</li>
	<li><strong>Internal Events &amp; Onsite Logistics:</strong> Coordinate logistics and day-of execution for onsite gatherings, executive visits, team offsites, and cultural celebrations in partnership with Global Events and IT.</li>
	<li><strong>Safety, Compliance &amp; Emergency Readiness:</strong> Coordinate onsite facility safety and compliance in partnership with Global Physical Security and property management, ensuring alignment with Singapore WSH risk assessments and SCDF emergency response standards.</li>
	<li><strong>Team Direction &amp; Site Support:</strong> Supervise day-to-day workflows for workplace contractors and coordinators, serving as the local operational partner for regional real estate project refreshes and regional coverage.</li>
	<li><strong>Regional Collaboration:</strong> Participate actively in regional operations syncs, sharing local operational insights, supporting peer site coverage across APJC, and ensuring consistent policy rollouts within the region.</li>
</ul>
<h2>Requirements</h2>
<ul>
	<li><strong>5+ years of workplace or facilities operations experience</strong>, preferably within a high-growth tech or fast-paced corporate environment.</li>
	<li><strong>1–2+ years of supervisory or team lead experience</strong>, managing onsite workplace staff, coordinators, or contractor teams.</li>
	<li><strong>Proven vendor management and RFP experience</strong>, including running competitive bidding, monitoring SLAs, managing partner relationships, and driving contract renewal reviews.</li>
	<li><strong>Operational budget tracking skills</strong>, with practical experience reviewing vendor invoices, managing POs, and monitoring site OpEx.</li>
	<li><strong>Cross-functional communication skills</strong>, with the ability to communicate operational updates confidently across time zones using Google Workspace and modern ticketing platforms.</li>
	<li><strong>Knowledge of Singapore safety standards</strong>, including MOM Workplace Safety and Health (WSH) guidelines and emergency response protocols.</li>
	<li><strong>Hospitality mindset &amp; hands-on problem solving</strong>, dedicated to delivering high operational standards in an employee-centric environment.</li>
	<li><strong>Onsite presence:</strong> Required to be onsite full-time, 5 days per week at our Singapore office.</li>
</ul>
