<h3>About the role</h3>
<p>Cloudflare's Digital Marketing team is building a unified, signal-driven demand engine. It recognizes accounts at the web edge, acts on real-time intent, and turns anonymous traffic into high-velocity pipeline. That engine is only as strong as the systems underneath it, and those systems need an owner.</p>
<p>We're hiring a&nbsp;<strong>Senior Marketing Systems and Automation Manager</strong> to be that owner. The heart of this role is running and governing marketing operations as they exist today: the intake queue, campaign operations, and lead flow that keeps demand generation moving, while steadily replacing the manual parts with automation. You have to understand the operation deeply, because you will both run it and decide what to automate. The systems architecture underneath is a real and important part of the job, and you will own and shape it in partnership with Engineering and Digital Marketing leaders, but your day-to-day is operating, governing, and automating the engine.</p>
<p>The through-line is an idea we care about a great deal. We want automation that creates global consistency without gates. Picture one standard way of working that every team can trust, with enough flexibility built in that a team in another region, or a specialist with a particular use case, can apply it to their own needs without breaking the standard. Getting that balance right is the heart of this job.</p>
<p>You'll turn a stack that runs today as a manual service function into an orchestrated engine, with Cloudflare's internal orchestration platform (Cloudflare OS) as the central system. You'll report directly to the Senior Director of Digital Marketing and lead a distributed contractor bench that spans architecture, development, and execution. You'll also be the senior counterpart to our BizApps, IT, Engineering, and Business Intelligence partners, and the single point of accountability for how marketing's systems are run, integrated, automated, and governed.</p>
<p>The person we're looking for respects the old world and is energized to build the new one. If you can run the operation that exists while designing the automation that replaces its manual parts, we want to talk to you.</p>
<h2>What you'll do</h2>
<p><strong>Run the marketing operations engine</strong></p>
<ul>
	<li>Own the day-to-day operation that keeps demand generation moving: the intake queue, campaign operations, lead flow, and provisioning.</li>
	<li>Direct a distributed contractor bench spanning lifecycle architecture, development, and execution, setting priorities, scope, and the quality bar.</li>
	<li>Keep the lights on with rigor while you steadily automate the parts that don't need a human, so nothing degrades during the transition.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>&nbsp;</strong></p>
<p><strong>Own governance and the systems standard</strong></p>
<ul>
	<li>Own data governance and the standards that keep the stack coherent globally: taxonomy, naming, integration patterns, and access.</li>
	<li>Own the lead scoring, lifecycle, and attribution model as a documented, version-controlled source of truth that operates as shared context for the company.</li>
	<li>Define and govern logic change controls, including who, how, and with what review.</li>
	<li>Create a transparent, auditable model that is portable to skills and shared context in Cloudflare OS.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>&nbsp;</strong></p>
<p><strong>Identify and drive agentic automation</strong></p>
<ul>
	<li>Make Cloudflare OS the orchestration hub for marketing operations, turning intake, provisioning, data hygiene, consent, and reporting from manual human queues into automated, governed workflows.</li>
	<li>Decide what should be automated and what genuinely needs a human, then scope, prioritize, and drive the build.</li>
	<li>Design automation that delivers global consistency without gates, so every team works from the same standard that adapts to local needs.</li>
	<li>Move the operation to be system-based, measuring the reduction in manual effort, without losing the operational discipline that keeps demand flowing today.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>&nbsp;</strong></p>

</ul>
