<p><strong>About the role</strong></p>
<p>Cloudforce One is Cloudflare’s threat operations and research team, responsible for identifying and disrupting cyber threats ranging from sophisticated cyber criminal activity to nation-state sponsored advanced persistent threats (APTs). Members of Cloudforce One are at the helm of leveraging an incredibly vast and varied set of data points that only one of the world’s largest global networks can provide. The team is able to analyze these unique data points, at massive scale and efficiency, synthesizing findings into actionable threat intelligence to better protect our customers.</p>
<p>You will join the data science team within Cloudforce One. We power much of the insight and intelligence delivered in Cloudflare’s <a href="https://developers.cloudflare.com/security-center/cloudforce-one/#analyze-threat-events">Threat Events</a> product, which customers can use to better protect themselves — for example, by <a href="https://blog.cloudflare.com/realtime-threat-intel-waf-rules/">turning our threat indicators into real-time WAF rules</a>. We own data pipelines that power analysis of Cloudflare’s DDoS and WAF data, including Cloudflare’s quarterly <a href="https://blog.cloudflare.com/ddos-threat-report-2026-q3/">DDoS Threat Report</a>. We also support internal Trust &amp; Safety initiatives such as identifying and addressing network abuse, and collaborate directly with the Threat Intelligence team.</p>
<p>As a Data Engineer, you will build the data foundation that makes this possible: designing and operating the scalable pipelines, analytical data stores, and data marts that turn raw Cloudflare telemetry into trusted, well-modeled datasets for threat researchers, data scientists, analysts, and customers.</p>
<p>This role may require flexibility to be on-call outside of standard working hours to address technical issues as needed.</p>
<h4><strong>What you'll do</strong></h4>
<ul>
	<li>Design, build, and operate scalable batch data pipelines on Kubernetes-hosted Airflow and Spark clusters that extract, enrich, and publish threat and abuse signals from Cloudflare’s network and platform telemetry.</li>
	<li>Build and evolve our data stores in Apache Iceberg (R2) and the curated data marts that serve Threat Events, Trust &amp; Safety initiatives, and Threat Intelligence investigations.</li>
	<li>Model datasets deliberately — choosing grain, partitioning, and schema evolution strategies that keep data correct, queryable, and cost-efficient.</li>
	<li>Write and optimize SQL against large-scale analytical data in ClickHouse, balancing completeness, performance, and cost.</li>
	<li>Make pipelines safe to retry, resume, and backfill, so downstream consumers receive exactly the data we intend to publish.</li>
	<li>Build data quality checks, reconciliation, and monitoring that catch problems before they reach analysts, detections, or customers.</li>
	<li>Treat privacy and data minimization as design requirements from the start.</li>
	<li>Enable Threat Intelligence and Trust &amp; Safety partners with the data they need for tooling and investigations, translating ad hoc requests into reusable datasets.</li>
	<li>Partner with data scientists to turn exploratory analysis into repeatable, production-quality data products and ML-ready datasets.</li>
	<li>Raise the team’s engineering bar through code review, testing, CI/CD, and documentation.</li>
</ul>
<h4><strong>Desirable Skills, Knowledge, and Experience:</strong></h4>
<ul>
	<li>5+ years of experience designing, building, and operating scalable production data pipelines.</li>
	<li>Proven experience building analytical data stores and data marts, including data modeling, partitioning, and schema evolution.</li>
	<li>Strong Python for data engineering, including testing and maintainable, well-structured code.</li>
	<li>Strong SQL, including window functions, query optimization, and performance tuning on large datasets.</li>
	<li>Hands-on experience with Apache Airflow in production, including Kubernetes-hosted deployments.</li>
	<li>Experience with distributed data processing (e.g. Spark or similar) at large scale.</li>
	<li>Experience with Apache Iceberg (or a comparable open table format) on object storage such as R2 or S3.</li>
	<li>Experience with ClickHouse or a comparable columnar analytical database.</li>
	<li>Experience with CI/CD, version control, and containerized deployments.</li>
	<li>Clear communication and the ability to turn ambiguous requirements from researchers and analysts into well-defined data products.</li>
</ul>
