### Cloudflare achieved global government certifications

**Federal Risk and Authorization Management Program** 

FedRAMP authorized cloud services for the U.S. government and is vital for research institutions subject to CMMC compliance. Cloudflare achieved a FedRAMP High authorization. 

**Infosec Registered Assessors Program** 

The IRAP security assessment helps Australian organizations validate that vital security controls are in place. Cloudflare has been assessed at the IRAP Protected level for high-impact, non-secret government data. 

**Information System Security Management and Assessment Program** 

ISMAP is a rigorous security baseline that supports Japan's commitment to cybersecurity compliance. Cloudflare successfully completed ISMAP Registration, demonstrating Cloudflare's commitment to Japan's Public Sector 

**Esquema Nacional de Seguridad** 

The National Security Framework (ENS) establishes key security requirements for public sector information systems in Spain. Cloudflare for Government achieved an ENS Certificate of Conformity at the High security level.
