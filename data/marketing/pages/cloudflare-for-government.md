## Cloudflare achieved global government certifications

###### Federal Risk and Authorization Management Program

FedRAMP authorized cloud services for the U.S. government and is vital for research institutions subject to CMMC compliance.

Cloudflare achieved a FedRAMP High authorization.

[Learn more](https://www.cloudflare.com/federal/)

###### Infosec Registered Assessors Program

The IRAP security assessment helps Australian organizations validate that vital security controls are in place.

Cloudflare has been assessed at the IRAP Protected level for high-impact, non-secret government data.

[Learn more](https://www.cloudflare.com/cloudflare-for-government/australia/)

###### Information System Security Management and Assessment Program

ISMAP is a rigorous security baseline that supports Japan's commitment to cybersecurity compliance.

Cloudflare successfully completed ISMAP Registration, demonstrating Cloudflare's commitment to Japan's public sector.

[Learn more](https://www.cloudflare.com/press/press-releases/2026/cloudflare-successfully-completes-ismap-registration-to-support-japans/)

###### Esquema Nacional de Seguridad

The National Security Framework (ENS) establishes key security requirements for public sector information systems in Spain.

Cloudflare for Government achieved an ENS Certificate of Conformity at the High security level.

[Learn more](https://www.cloudflare.com/trust-hub/compliance-resources/ens/)
