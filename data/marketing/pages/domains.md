## Frequently asked questions

 What is Cloudflare Registrar? 

 Cloudflare Registrar allows users to register, renew, and manage domain names without hidden fees or inflated renewal costs. Cloudflare Registrar also offers defenses against domain hijacking and natively integrates DNSSEC for additional domain security. 

 How does Cloudflare Registrar offer domains at cost? 

 Cloudflare Registrar does not mark up domain prices at all. Cloudflare ensures customers only pay the price charged by registries and ICANN for domain registration and renewal, eliminating extra fees when buying a domain. 

 What security features does Cloudflare Registrar provide for domains? 

 Cloudflare Registrar is natively integrated with our security services like DNS, CDN, and SSL services. It also offers free DNSSEC as well as advanced features such as registry lock and out-of-band auth to prevent hijacking. 

 Can I transfer my existing domains to Cloudflare Registrar? 

 Yes, you can easily transfer and manage your existing domains through the Cloudflare Dashboard. 

 What is DNSSEC and how does Cloudflare Registrar support it? 

 DNSSEC uses cryptographic signatures to prevent domain hijacking and on-path attacks. Cloudflare Registrar makes it easy and free, offering universal, one-click DNSSEC activation for all your domains. 

 What types of top-level domains (TLDs) does Cloudflare Registrar support? 

 Cloudflare Registrar supports over 430 top-level domains, from popular ones like .com, .net, and .org to niche TLDs like .ai, .app and .dev. [See supported domain extensions](#supported-domain-extensions) 

 How do I submit a WHOIS Data Disclosure Request? 

 To submit a WHOIS Data Disclosure Request, [  complete this form ](https://abuse.cloudflare.com/registrar%5Fwhois). 

 What are my rights and responsibilities as a registrant? 

 To learn more about your rights and responsibilities, please review the [  Cloudflare Domain Registration Agreement ](https://cloudflare.com/domain-registration-agreement) and [  ICANN's Registrants' Benefits and Responsibilities page ](https://www.icann.org/resources/pages/benefits-2013-09-16-en).
