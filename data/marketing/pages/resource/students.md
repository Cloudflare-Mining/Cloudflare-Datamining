##  Cloudflare for Students 

 Build on Cloudflare's developer platform — the serverless platform for building, deploying, and scaling applications. 

 Eligible students in the US with a valid .edu email address can receive one year of Cloudflare Workers with upfront costs waived. Get everything included in Workers Free, plus: 

######  Deploy up to 500 Worker Scripts 

######  10 million requests/month (+ $0.30/million additional requests), with up to 30s of CPU time per request 

######  20 million included logs/month (+$0.60/million additional logs) 

######  6,000 included build minutes per month ($0.005/minute additional) 

######  6 concurrent build slots 

######  Additional Workers KV edge storage 

######  After 12 months, you can easily renew your subscription by upgrading to our Workers Paid plan. If you choose not to, your account will automatically revert to the free plan, and you won't be charged.

### How to get started

**1 Sign up for a free account** 

Sign up for a free account Cloudflare with your .edu email address verified. Existing eligible .edu users with verified emails can proceed to the next step. 

**2 Add a payment method** 

In the Billing page, and add a valid credit card and confirm that your billing email address is your .edu email. 

**3 Add Workers Paid to your account to redeem** 

Visit our Workers Plans page to add the Workers Paid plan to your account. The $5/mo fee will be automatically waived for 12 months. 

**4 Check your inbox** 

Check your email for a confirmation that your account has redeemed the offer.

### What happens when I'm at the end of the 12 months?

If you're at the end of your 12 months, you can renew your subscription by upgrading your plan to the Workers Paid plan. If you don't upgrade, by default your account will revert to our free plan.

![Background Pattern](/static/pattern.png)2

### Who is eligible for the Cloudflare for Students paid developer features offering?

The current offering is available only to students in the United States over the age of 18 who have a verified .edu billing email address. Please review Cloudflare for Students terms below for detailed eligibility criteria.

![Background Pattern](/static/pattern.png)3

### Why is the program limited to .edu (US students) only?

This is just the first step for Cloudflare for Students. We are working on expanding support to students in other countries. If you are a student outside of the US, you can request for your educational institution to be added here.

![Background Pattern](/static/pattern.png)4

### What is the difference between our Workers Free Plan and the Workers Paid Plan?

The Workers Paid plan comes with increased usage limits and features compared to the free plan and is ideal for students to start building scalable applications.

![Background Pattern](/static/pattern.png)5
