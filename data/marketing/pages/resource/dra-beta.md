 DATA SOVEREIGNTY 

##  Data Residency Architecture private beta 

 Data sovereignty is an important consideration for many organizations buying cloud services. But too often, sovereign cloud services come with critical tradeoffs: reduced resilience and performance, management inflexibility, or worse visibility into global threats. 

 You shouldn't have to live with these tradeoffs in order to meet your data sovereignty needs. Cloudflare's Data Residency Architecture offers a better path forward, letting you enforce rigorous sovereignty controls while still benefiting from the scale, security, and reliability of a global cloud network.
