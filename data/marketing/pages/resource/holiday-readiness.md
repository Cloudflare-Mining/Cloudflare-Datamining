##  Your holiday checklist has a new line item: AI agents 

 The Internet is being hit harder and faster than ever before. Cloudflare now blocks over 5,300 DDoS attacks every hour — most are over in under 10 minutes, too fast for any human to respond. 2025 was a record. 2026 is already outpacing it. But this holiday season, it's not just the attacks that are agentic — the shopping is too. AI assistants are now deciding which products to recommend, which stores to trust, and which to skip. You need to block the bots that attack you and show up for the ones that sell for you.

### Six moves to make before peak:

**Absorb DDoS at the edge** 

DDoS Protection – Autonomous protection on a 500 Tbps network blocks multi-terabit floods in seconds. 

**Manage the surge** 

Waiting Room – A branded virtual waiting room keeps checkout stable during flash sales and drops. 

**Sort good bots from bad** 

Bot Management – ML bot scoring plus session analysis; verify trusted agents via Web Bot Auth. 

**Stop fraud without friction** 

WAF + Turnstile – Holiday-tuned rules catch abuse; Turnstile confirms real users without CAPTCHAs. 

**Secure APIs & checkout** 

API Shield + Page Shield – Discover and protect every API and lock down client-side scripts against skimming. 

**Show up for AI shoppers** 

Agent Readiness – Make your catalog discoverable and serve clean, machine-readable content.
