# NYC Department of Buildings 

> #### "  We went through a vulnerability report line by line, writing rules to remediate these vulnerabilities. Some of these rules had to be two-fold or three-fold and act more like an application than a regular firewall would. " 

## About the NYC DOB

NYC Department of Buildings (DOB) is responsible for ensuring the safe and lawful use of buildings in New York City. This includes verifying that construction plans meet code requirements, inspecting the work, and intervening in illegal construction. The agency is responsible for approximately 5 million buildings and has thousands of active permits at all times.

## Securing a legacy, vulnerable application

The NYC DOB operates a public-facing, legacy application that contained multiple vulnerabilities and is critical to the agency's mission of approving and conducting construction inspections. Since the application was no longer supported, addressing the vulnerabilities within the application itself would have required hiring a consultant to study, fix, and test the application. "With Cloudflare, we were able to validate that the Cloudflare WAF would solve our problem in a POC," Doga Ozturk, NYCDOB's Executive Directo of Information Security and Infrastructure, explained. "They performed a lot of white glove knowledge transfer to ensure that we could build and maintain the custom rules we needed."

## Integrated vulnerability management and load balancing for public-facing apps

The NYCDOB worked with Cloudflare to develop custom rules to virtually patch more than ten vulnerabilities in the end-of-life application, including multiple ones of Critical severity. "We went through a vulnerability report line by line, writing rules to remediate these vulnerabilities," Ozturk recalled. "Some of these rules had to be two-fold or three-fold and act more like an application than a regular firewall would."

As part of the transition to Cloudflare, the NYCDOB also needed to migrate from an end-of-life load balancer. After several months of working with an on-prem solution, the agency sought approval to use Cloudflare's integrated load balancer. "Within three weeks, we were able to get Cloudflare's load balancer approved and deployed versus spending five months failing to get the on-prem load balancer to work," Ozturk commented. "Initially, we engaged Cloudflare for its WAF, and solving our load balancer problem through the same platform is awesome." NYCDOB was also one of the first New York City agency to implement cloud managed load balancer for on prem webservers.

With Cloudflare WAF and load balancer, NYCDOB also received an unprecedented level of visibility into how users interact with their application. "Now, we can see how the web sees the application versus how we do," Ozturk explained. "It enhances how we do operations from the application perspective." Now, the team can identify and investigate anomalous traffic and can rate limit high-volume, automated traffic to ensure equal access for all of its users.

## Ensuring application performance and security

With custom rules in the Cloudflare WAF, the agency didn't need to spend money securing the code of an end-of-life application. Additionally, the visibility provided by the Cloudflare Dashboard reassured the team that the migration was successful and didn't cause any loss of service. "I could task one engineer with checking the traffic levels on the application to validate that everything was working properly," Ozturk recalled. "I didn't need to have a team of people going over everything to ensure that we didn't max out a server or drop requests."

## Providing secure and reliable public services

Keeping the NYCDOB's public facing applications secure is Ozturk's primary responsibility. Cloudflare both protects against attacks and provides the visibility needed for troubleshooting any problems. He commented, "Beyond the security benefits, I can open one dashboard and see more than I ever could before, which is an invaluable tool for supporting our mission."
